Link two accounts

The main account keeps everything and is the one the person signs in as. The other account is kept but made inactive, signed out everywhere, and can no longer sign in as itself. Nothing is deleted. Its address becomes an address of the main account, so its tickets and followed tickets show in the main account's My requests.

What happens

  • The {{ secondary.assigned_tickets }} ticket{{ "" if secondary.assigned_tickets == 1 else "s" }} assigned to {{ secondary.user.email }} move to {{ main.user.email }}, each with a ticket event. Messages, files, follows, notifications and other records that name the other account move too.
  • Audit and activity rows keep the original account.
  • Sessions of {{ secondary.user.email }} end and the account becomes inactive. Its unanswered approval votes are dropped.
  • You can unlink later; tickets that moved stay where they are.
