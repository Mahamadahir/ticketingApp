Treat as empty

Lines above a forwarded email that do not count as something the forwarder wrote.

When someone inside the organisation forwards an email to the support mailbox, the forward rules look at what they wrote above the forwarded message. Nothing written means the person who sent the original is the requester; any words mean the forwarder is. Mail programs add lines of their own (such as "Sent from my iPhone"), so a line that matches an enabled rule below does not count as writing. Lines are compared in lower case with punctuation removed, and a pattern may use * as a wildcard (for example sent from outlook for*). Regular expressions are not supported.

Also never counted, without any rule: the forwarder's own signature, meaning a line that is only their name, job title or department from the directory, a line that is only a bracketed picture name such as [Vardags], and a line that starts with {% for label in labels %}{{ label }}:{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. Only the forward decision is affected: the email is stored and shown whole, and Thanks rules are separate.
