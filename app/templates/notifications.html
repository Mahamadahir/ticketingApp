Notifications

When a ticket is assigned to you, or someone mentions you in an internal note (write @ and the part of your email address before the @), or when a request is waiting for your approval (that one opens in the portal, where you vote). Everyone is also told about their own tickets, the ones they raised or follow from outside the team: when IT Support emails you, the ticket is waiting for your reply, resolved or closed, or your request is approved or declined (these open in the portal; the emails are sent as well). Choose which of these you want under My notification settings. For tickets you work as an agent, you are only told about tickets you can open; if a ticket moves to a team you are not in, its notifications are hidden (and not counted) until you can open it again.
