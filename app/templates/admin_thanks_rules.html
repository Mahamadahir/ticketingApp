Thanks rules

Replies on a resolved ticket that only say thanks, and so do not reopen it.

A reply on a resolved ticket normally reopens it. If the new text of the reply (not the quoted thread) matches an enabled rule below and the reply has no attachment, it is recorded and the ticket stays resolved. Anything else reopens. Replies are compared in lower case with punctuation and emoji removed, so "Thanks!! :)" is "thanks", and the maximum length counts those remaining characters. A pattern may use * as a wildcard (for example thanks for*resolving). Regular expressions are not supported. Rules apply to portal comments as well as email.
