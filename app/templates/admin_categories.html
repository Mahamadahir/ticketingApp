A main category belongs to a team; a sub-category uses its parent's team. "Other" is for tickets nothing fits: they are worked normally, then reviewed under Category review after they are resolved. Switching a category off hides it from triage; tickets already in it are untouched.

"Escalates to" is where the Escalate button sends a triaged ticket in that category. Every category starts on the MSP; a sub-category follows its main category unless you pick something else. Escalating to the MSP is Send to MSP: the ticket stays in its team with the status With MSP (set the MSP address under Settings first). If the ticket is already with the chosen team, the next step is the MSP.
