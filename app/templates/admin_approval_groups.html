Approval groups

A group is the set of people who vote on a kind of request. A request type (Admin > Request types) points at one group. Members need an account (they sign in once first); they need not be agents, managers are typical. When a request is triaged, the members at that moment become its voters and the number is fixed: adding or removing people later does not change a request already waiting, although someone removed can no longer answer it. A super admin can waive a request that can then never be decided.
