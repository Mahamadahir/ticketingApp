Never-follow list

Addresses that must never be copied on a ticket: the shared mailbox and its aliases, and distribution lists. A Cc of a listed address on inbound mail is skipped, and nobody can add one as a follower by hand. Addresses are matched exactly (upper or lower case makes no difference); a whole domain cannot be listed. Removing an address allows it again, and followers already on tickets are left as they are.
