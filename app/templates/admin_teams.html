Team links

By default a team sees only its own tickets. A link lets agents of one team see (viewer) or work (member) another team's tickets. It is one-way and does not chain; for both directions add two links. A team's "Escalates to" team counts as a link at member level. Agents who are only viewers of their own team never get more than view access through a link.
