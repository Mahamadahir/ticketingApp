Data protection

Subject access exports and anonymising a requester.

Subject access export

Everything held on one person, as a ZIP. Enter any address they have; their linked addresses and old addresses are included. The ZIP holds the account record, the tickets they raised, follow or are copied on (the messages the requester may see, plus their form answers, rating and comments), approval votes, notifications, preferences, sign-in activity, audit entries, and the files they sent. Internal notes are in a separate file, to decide on before anything is disclosed. Staff work is listed by reference only.

The file is built when you press the button, sent to you and then deleted: it is not kept on the server and there is no link to share. Each export is recorded in the audit log. Attachments are added up to {{ max_mb }} MB (Admin > Settings); anything over is listed with its size.

Anonymise a requester

Keeps their tickets and removes the person: name, email address, files, what they wrote and what was emailed to them. Enter any address they have; their linked and old addresses are included. You see exactly what will change before anything does. This cannot be undone. Agents and super admins are not anonymised here.
